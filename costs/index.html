São Paulo operating costs

The estimate uses 730 hours per month, USD on-demand rates and the November 20, 2026 inventory in sa-east-1. The four public IPv4 addresses cover the existing gateway, the two teammates’ public hosts and the NAT gateway. The RDS instance has seven-day backup retention. These are list-price projections before credits, taxes, usage-based charges and discounts.

An internet-facing load balancer with two public IPv4 addresses adds about USD 7.30/month. An internal load balancer uses private addresses. NAT adds USD 0.093 per GB processed. Load-balancer capacity or processed bytes, registry storage, cross-AZ traffic, internet egress, burst credits and excess backup storage depend on usage. Managed services and availability

RDS manages PostgreSQL backups and patching. EKS operates the Kubernetes control plane. The group remains responsible for application rollouts, database migrations, worker capacity and recovery. The EC2 app has single service hosts and a single-AZ database. A database outage can stop purchases even when Gateway and Exchange are healthy.

A proposed course-demo objective is 99% successful authenticated reads per month and p95 below 500 ms for cached catalog reads at ten concurrent users. Count unexpected 5xx responses and timeouts as failures. Exclude deliberately invalid test requests. At 99% availability a 30-day month allows 7 hours 12 minutes of downtime. The short local measurements support the latency target under that workload. A monthly availability claim needs continuous end-to-end measurements. Load-test results
