Pato Nego

A buyer registers through Gateway, signs in, and orders products priced in US dollars. The order belongs to that buyer. When the buyer asks for a total in Brazilian reais, Order calls Exchange for the Banco Central do Brasil PTAX sell rate. The stored price stays in dollars.

Demonstrated purchase

On November 20, 2026, the public EC2 and EKS gateways each accepted an order for two units at USD 4.25. The order list and detail both showed USD 8.50. At the quoted rate of 5.8889, Order returned BRL 50.06. A second signed-in account received 404 for the same order and had an empty order list. The test accounts and product were removed after the check.

Watch all six APIs running in EKS and the purchase through the public gateway. The load-test recording shows Gateway autoscaling under load.

Services and source

Responsibility Repository
Project integration and Compose nego-pato
Authenticate and route traffic Gateway-service
Register users, issue and validate login cookies Auth-service and Auth contract
Store accounts and password hashes in PostgreSQL Account-service and Account contract
Fetch and cache PTAX quotes Exchange
Store and cache catalog products Product
Save orders, enforce ownership and convert totals Order-service and Order contract
